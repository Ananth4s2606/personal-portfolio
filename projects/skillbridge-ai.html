SkillBridge AI

An AI-powered career intelligence and skill verification platform concept that helps students identify skill gaps, verify abilities and build a trusted Skill Passport.

  • AI
  • Career Intelligence
  • EdTech
  • Skill Verification
  • Prototype, in progress

Problem

Students often list skills based on self-assessment, while recruiters may not have enough evidence to determine whether those skills are demonstrated.

Solution

SkillBridge AI combines skill assessment, practical verification, project evidence and AI-assisted evaluation to distinguish self-declared skills from verified skills.

Main features

  • Skill Assessment
  • Skill Gap Analysis
  • Learning Roadmap
  • Skill Verification
  • AI Interview
  • Trust Score
  • Skill Passport
  • Career Intelligence
  • Recruiter View
